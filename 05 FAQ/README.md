## FAQ | Frequently Asked Questions

To better support ongoing chip development technical services, it is recommended to maintain a dedicated FAQ section in this repository.

The FAQ is intended to collect and reuse solutions to recurring development issues, especially in areas such as:

- DARIC chip usage and configuration
- EVB bring-up and peripheral validation
- RISC-V development environment and toolchain setup
- Download/flash tool failures and recovery steps
- Version compatibility across SDK, firmware, and tools

By continuously updating FAQ entries during support and development activities, teams can reduce repeated troubleshooting effort, shorten response time, and improve onboarding efficiency for new developers.

> Suggested structure: topic-based files (e.g., `chip.md`, `evb.md`, `riscv-dev.md`, `download-tool.md`)
